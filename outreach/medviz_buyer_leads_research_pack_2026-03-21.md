## Deliverability Reality Check
- Technical baseline passes for all five addresses (valid domain + MX present).
- Practical deliverability can still fail because of mailbox-level blocks, strict spam filters, or role inbox policies.
- Recommended send strategy:
  1. Send 5-message micro-batch first.
  2. Wait 24-48 hours.
  3. Remove hard-bounce addresses and tighten copy for non-repliers.

## Personalization Approach Used
- Segment-level but source-backed personalization (services, team structure, referral workflow, clinical focus, location context).
- Avoided invented personal claims and private details.
- Kept single CTA: 15-minute workflow call for a $1,000, 7-day pilot sprint.
